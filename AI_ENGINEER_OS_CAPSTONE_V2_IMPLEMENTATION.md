# Capstone V2 Implementation

## Delivered

- Versioned `capstoneSelection` settings with explicit product and deep-build track identifiers.
- Missing selection data retains the legacy V1 behavior for saved roadmaps.
- New onboarding plans always include four required portfolio projects and allow selection of 13 other products and nine deep-build tracks.
- The scheduler adds the documented prerequisite closure, respects known-topic satisfaction, and preserves manual inclusion/exclusion precedence.
- Selection data is retained by generation, rescheduling, track previews, persistence, reload, and cloud-synchronized roadmap state.
- The final onboarding step shows required work, selectable work, duration per choice, and a live full-plan estimate generated by the production scheduler.

## Required Baseline

1. Terminal-Native Coding Agent
2. RAG Over a Codebase
3. Production RAG Chatbot
4. LLM Observability Dashboard

Direct baseline workload: 7,200 minutes (120 hours).
